ABC Technologies

ABC Technologies is a software company founded in 2015.

The company provides services in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science,
Cloud Computing, and Software Development.

The company headquarters is located in Bengaluru, India.

ABC Technologies has approximately 500 employees.

The company offers three main products:

1. AI Analytics Platform
2. Cloud Management System
3. Customer Support Automation Platform

The AI Analytics Platform helps businesses analyze large amounts of data
and generate useful business insights.

The Cloud Management System helps organizations monitor and manage
their cloud infrastructure.

The Customer Support Automation Platform uses artificial intelligence
to automate customer queries and support operations.

The company provides training programs for fresh graduates in Python,
SQL, Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence.

The company's working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
